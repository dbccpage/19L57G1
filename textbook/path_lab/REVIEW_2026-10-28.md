AuthorityEffect: None. This visual prerequisite route stays outside the seal.

## Teaching change

Seven short lessons keep four vertices and five selectable edges visible
while introducing a graph, walk, path, closed walk, integer endpoint tally,
face guard, signed boundary, and a sum of faces. Five deterministic SVGs
appear directly in the written lessons. The interactive desk uses the same
recorded cases, with separately predicted face admission and walk validity.

The main example adds ABC and ACD. Their opposite AC contributions cancel,
while the edge stays admitted. Reversing ACD makes the AC coefficient −2
without changing cell membership. A new K/L/M/N practice case has equal
default shared-edge signs, requiring the reader to apply the convention
rather than memorize which edge disappears from a tally.

No face follows from a closed route alone. A face step needs its actual
facet guard and event. A zero endpoint tally retains no full walk history.
An all-zero double boundary does not imply a well-formed cell: the raw
repeated list A,A,B cancels but fails the guard. The source's all-positive
deletion control gives +2 at every triangle vertex. It changes both
boundary applications and is distinguished from reversing one edge.
