# Path lab: when does a closed route bound a face?

Begin after the [Reasoning lab](../reasoning_lab/README.md). This route
introduces points, connections, routes, signed counts, and faces before
the longer book uses them. No earlier geometry or algebra is assumed.

Keep four named points A, B, C, D throughout. Two candidate triangles,
ABC and ACD, share the connection AC. Predict what a drawn connection,
a closed route, and a separately admitted face each supply.

| Lesson | One question |
|---|---|
| [00. Read a graph](00_points_and_connections.md) | What does a line in this diagram record? |
| [01. Follow a walk](01_follow_a_walk.md) | Can we take every step we have written down? |
| [02. Count arrivals and departures](02_signed_endpoint_counts.md) | What cancels at an intermediate point? |
| [03. Closed does not mean filled](03_closed_and_filled.md) | Does returning to A admit a triangular face? |
| [04. Take a face's boundary](04_a_faces_boundary.md) | Why do the three signed edges balance at their endpoints? |
| [05. Join two faces](05_join_two_faces.md) | What happens to the shared connection AC? |
| [06. Keep the convention visible](06_conventions_and_scope.md) | Which rule makes cancellation true, and what does it leave unproved? |
