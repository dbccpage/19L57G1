## What the reader can now inspect

Eight short lessons carry a two-entry separation demand from its supplied
ledger through forms and instances, all well-formed plans, both cost
rules, all tied minima, explicit isomorphism maps, protection and budget
changes on a three-entry ledger, stopping reasons, and portable replay.
Practice uses a fresh labeled ledger with separate worked answers.

The results desk requires a prediction before revealing a recorded case.
It displays all fourteen well-formed two-item plans in the default space,
including eight failures. Changing price or arity selects the corresponding
recorded result. Protection and item budget are independent controls.
The displayed repair table separates demand satisfaction from whether a
plan fits the selected budget. It performs no Python search or replay
in the browser, and says so explicitly.

The longer normalized graph has 128 nodes, 130 edges, and three merge
edges. Replay rejects a wrong root fingerprint and missing merge map,
but accepts changed price metadata. The separate full record check
rejects that price change. This exact distinction is taught in Lesson 07.
