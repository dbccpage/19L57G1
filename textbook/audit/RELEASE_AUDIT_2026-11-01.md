# Textbook release audit

2026-11-01. AuthorityEffect: None.

## Decision

The requested textbook revision is ready for publication as an editorial
release. It retains the full 75-Unit derivation curriculum, eighteen
Institution Units, the beginner course, mathematical toolkits and selected
research introductions. The review does not redefine the book as only its
entrance course. PUB-01 closes after the final status-page publication and
preservation check recorded in the handoff receipt.

The author reported reading the material and finding it acceptable. That
acceptance is retained; no new participant is required to finish this text.
The historical pilot timings and scores were proposed research targets,
not observed outcomes. This release makes no measured learning-speed,
other-reader mastery or delayed-retention claim. Its exercises let readers
test and explain changed examples without treating those answers as
measurements of every reader's future performance.

## Entrance abilities have concrete places to learn and check them

The original proposed entrance tasks remain in the book:

- Label versus answer: First steps 00 and reader-check question 1.
- Collapse, separation and a witnessing test: First steps 01–03 and
  reader-check questions 2–3.
- Supplied assumptions and admission: First steps 04 and the core
  Reasoning hypothesis/evidence lessons.
- Different effects of renaming and adding a discriminating test:
  First steps 00/03 and changed-example questions 1/4.
- Recovery or a pair blocking it: First steps 02, reader-check question 5,
  and the formal-reading packet's different code/request table.

The packets ask for reasons and include unfamiliar transfer questions.
Their existence and checked answers are teaching evidence, not timed
reader-performance evidence. No twenty-minute or one-hour guarantee is made.

## What remains outside this release

- The author controls merging. PR #296 remains draft, directly against
  main, and unmerged. Run the full twelve-family local contract before
  asking for a merge; no hosted CI is used or expected.
- Exhaustive per-declaration teaching is not claimed. The qualified source
  index remains a guide; a future exhaustive-coverage claim would need
  additional reconciliation.
- Independent timing, retention and broader reader studies remain optional
  evidence for stronger learning claims. They are not invented here or
  reopened as a new participant requirement.
- Mathematical questions labelled open in the research remain open.
  Finishing the textbook does not turn those questions into proved results.
