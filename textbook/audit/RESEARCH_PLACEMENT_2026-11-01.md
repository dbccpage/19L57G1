# Research placement: the 123-tree editorial decision

2026-11-01. AuthorityEffect: None.

This table assigns teaching roles, not mathematical grades. Owner entry summaries and the existing curriculum were inspected; no new research build, full implementation audit or independent theorem verification was performed. Source-entry hashes and the exact population are in [the machine-readable table](research_placement.json).

## Selection rule

The beginner foundation uses the sealed-development examples and the existing First steps, Reasoning, Search and Change routes. A research tree is not a prerequisite merely because it exists. The original eighteen selected research introductions remain available; X302 and X303 now have their bounded introductions. Advanced mathematics, tooling and separate derivation histories stay accessible as references. An open-question role preserves the named unresolved boundary; it does not label every result of that tree unproved. An omission removes a standalone chapter, not the source or its inventory entry.

No research tree is newly assigned to the required entrance. This is a pedagogical decision about dependencies, not a claim that the core route is complete: the separate PATH-01 review records its selected-route reading. It does not remove any existing lesson or narrow the overall beginner-textbook goal.

These are elective for readers. EXT-01 and EXT-02 are now delivered at their stated selected-introduction scopes. They should be short additions to the existing Reasoning/Change continuation, not new laboratories or research campaigns.
