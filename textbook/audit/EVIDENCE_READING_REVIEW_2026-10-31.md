# From a model to a recorded trial: teaching extension

Date: 2026-10-31. AuthorityEffect: None.

## The gap addressed

The Reasoning lab's Lesson 07 distinguished definitions, deductions,
finite computations and measurements, but gave no complete worked record
through a mismatch, repeat, uncertain answer and scope change. This
extends that existing route with Lessons 08–11, changed-envelope practice
and a separate answer key, a desk section, and an optional paper worksheet.
It does not add a research tree or validate an empirical correspondence.

The four short lessons cover:

1. Fix a domain, procedure, conditions and answer-code legend before
   interpreting a record. A definition does not make a physical rule true.
2. A conditional and an interpreted Yes/No case cannot all hold. This
   logical incompatibility does not identify which application premise
   failed or supply a unique physical cause.
3. Keep conflicting repeats, unknown answers and out-of-scope rows. A
   deterministic reading indexed only by card differs from one indexed
   by trial; adding an index does not explain changing readings.
4. Collect readings only if the optional paper activity is actually
   performed. Keep predictions, raw rows, corrections and later trials
   separate, and state what remains unmeasured.

## Checks

The classifier treats ? as either possible Boolean value. An independent
completion oracle checks nine partial answer pairs across eight metadata
choices, giving 72 comparisons. Eligible outcomes are counterexample,
compatible or unresolved; a different protocol and an outside domain or
condition remain separate statuses. The twelve fixture rows include
six compatible, one counterexample, two unresolved, two outside scope,
and one protocol mismatch.

One known repeat conflict remains: `new-N` versus `repeat-N` on crease.
The partial `unclear-N` reading is not treated as a known difference or
as No. All raw rows remain intact.

Fifteen authoring tests passed. They include the finite completion check,
malformed-code and provenance rejection, source-map scope, stale packet
and desk detection, retention of repeated rows, and execution of the actual
evidence event-handler source in a minimal DOM fixture. The Node fixture
checks all twelve selected cases, prediction gating, incorrect feedback,
and reset after case/prediction changes. It supplies no browser layout or
accessibility evidence.

The book-Lean route now checks thirteen reports: ten empty axiom sets,
one `propext`, and two `propext, Quot.sound`. The added
`incompatible_application_bundle` report is axiom-free and proves only
the logical step from an implication and contradictory interpreted
premises. A false compatible-premise version is rejected and the original
driver recovers. Other negative checks are in the
[injection record](../../docs/injection_record.md).
