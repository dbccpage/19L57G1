## Exact source checks

`DiffInterface.DiffObs` has `delta`, `Zero : R → Prop`, and three proof
fields; it has no required `Decidable` field. A supplied proposition P
defines a valid book observer on Bool with difference report `(x,y)` and
zero condition `x = y ∨ P`. Constructing it needs no `Decidable P`.
Its off-diagonal zero question is equivalent to P, and an explicitly
supplied decider for that question gives a decider for P. This is a checked
interface example, not an undecidability theorem or a claim that particular
finite observers lack algorithms.

`BareReflection` uses two proof fields for parent/new inequality and three
pair fields for active obstruction. `ExtensionProposal` carries a request
kind and demanded relation, with no impossibility certificate. `drive`
returns `closed` on a supplied `none`. Its general termination theorem
assumes strict measure decrease on continuing steps; it imposes no general
equivalence between that stopping tag and an empty demand relation.

`ClosureStep` also permits an empty selected demand condition D and the
unchanged observer. The book constructs such an identity step for any
stage and supplied stage-map bundle. Naming a closure step therefore does
not establish strict progress; the decrease hypothesis is separate.

The reading guide's card table is explicitly encoded by native natural
numbers. `FactorsOn [0,1,2] stored requested` is false, while
`FactorsOn [0,2] stored requested` holds. The change of domain remains
part of the statement, and the lookup receives only stored labels.

`textbook/checks/u44_observer_spec_lean.py` checks eight reports against
the actual imported sealed definitions: four use no axioms and four use
only `propext`. The general observer definition, decider transfer, finite
lookup proof and generic-driver counterexample stay outside the sealed
census. No `Classical.choice` or proof escape is added.
