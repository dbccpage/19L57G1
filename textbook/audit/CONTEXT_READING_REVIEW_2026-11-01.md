## Semantic scope

Collapse compares answers and does not merge indices. A profile records
derivable pluggings; absence from that collection is part of a comparison,
not an admitted negative judgment or a physical measurement. Context
well-formedness checks filled slots, but does not validate the datum later
put in a hole. Native predicates accept natural-number arguments; an
application to admitted data must retain the range condition.

The old importance ranking and unqualified claims that no other sense of
difference occurs anywhere in the derivation are removed. The finite
Python enumeration is described with its actual model scope, without
promoting it to a theorem over Python source. The separation definition
does not itself supply a general permutation or physical-identity result.
