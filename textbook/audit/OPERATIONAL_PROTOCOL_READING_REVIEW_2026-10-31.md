Five boundaries matter for comprehension and accuracy:

1. The formal carrier has six unbounded `Nat` fields. Finite candidate
   populations do not make that type finite. Python's optional capacity
   check does not upper-bound the diagnostic field either.
2. Readout overwrites one mark. A record can become stale, and initial
   zero does not certify that a readout occurred. No timestamp or
   never-read tag exists in the model.
3. Leaving a target unchanged does not guarantee commutation with an
   operation that changes that same target. Conversely, overlap alone
   does not force failure. Lean proves named operation equations, not a
   generic theorem from arbitrary Python footprint metadata. The metadata
   and generic instrument flag each admit misleading configurations;
   the book retains counterexamples and restricts its desk operations.
4. Same-probe readouts agree on equal target values. Equal marks do not
   recover targets under a coarse probe, and different probes can disagree
   on equal targets. The source's asymmetric theorem is one copying
   exhibit; the book separately proves the conditional injective converse.
5. A diagnostic counter records a configured addition. It neither repairs
   different marks nor proves an unchanged target when its price is zero.
   Sending/receiving copies are explicit updates; a fresh readout is still
   needed to replace B's old record. The Python contrast helper constructs
   its own states, so the book does not present it as arbitrary-state replay.
