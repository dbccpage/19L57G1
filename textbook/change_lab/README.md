# Change lab: what lets a proposed repair happen?

Three entries, A, B, and C, stay on the page throughout this lab. We keep
separate records of what was asserted, what was retained, what must stay
distinguishable, and which questions can currently distinguish it.

Then we change one condition at a time. A repair may be possible but not
funded. A new question may be proposed but not permitted. A refusal may
be recorded without halting. Each claim has a small example to inspect.

Read [First steps](../first_steps/README.md) and the
[search lab](../search_lab/README.md) first. This chapter uses the existing
**passage** model, a different declared machine from the search ledger.
Its source names and exact boundaries are collected after the examples.
