# 07. Carry the claim with its model

[Previous](06_records_and_histories.md) · [Change lab](README.md) · [Practice](PRACTICE.md)

## Three questions about one proposed change

When someone says “this repair should work,” ask:

1. **Which model and starting state?** What are its entries, questions,
   protected demands, and event rules?
2. **Which condition is being claimed?** Is this a listed proposal,
   a structural repair, an executable event, or a resolved demand?
3. **What evidence fits that claim?** A definition, a finite calculation,
   a general theorem with hypotheses, or a measured response?

The passage desk makes these distinctions visible. An active pair can
have a structural repair. The repair can lack budget. An extension can
lack permission. A generated funding block can change the state without
resolving its target. A halt can leave the target unresolved.

## Same label, different rules

The search lab counted admission items and applied a separate response
filter. This passage stores a budget and uses guards inside its step.
One passage column event costs one budget unit. That is not automatically
the same price as a form-plus-instance plan in the search grammar.

The [model map](MODEL_MAP.md) also separates the two Lean ledgers,
the difference interface, the represented-world kernel, and institution
presentations. Moving a conclusion between them needs a specified map
and the relevant preservation result. Similar-looking tables do not
supply that result by themselves.

## Take a question into the world

Suppose a sorting system must preserve distinctions between parcels.
Record which distinctions the task needs. Find out what its sensors
actually report. If a proposed change adds a sensor, identify the
resources and authority required to install and use it.

Then test the resulting system on cases that could refute the intended
measurement correspondence. Repeated or noisy responses need their own
analysis. Our supplied membership columns give a reasoning pattern;
they provide no measurements of those parcels or sensors.
