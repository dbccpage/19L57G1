# Same-record lab: match answers without merging things

Two items can give the same answers to every question in a model and
still occupy different positions in its record. This route asks how to
make their answers match by adding information.

Begin after [First steps](../first_steps/README.md), the
[Reasoning lab](../reasoning_lab/README.md), and the
[Search lab](../search_lab/README.md). The
[Map lab](../map_lab/README.md) is useful for thinking about equal rows
and grouped items. No further mathematics is assumed.

| Lesson | One question |
|---|---|
| [00. Match the rows](00_match_the_rows.md) | What exactly becomes equal? |
| [01. Compare two small replies](01_two_small_replies.md) | Can equally cheap replies have different side effects? |
| [02. Read a question with two places](02_questions_with_two_places.md) | How can an addition about A change B's answers? |
| [03. Keep the promise and the budget separate](03_promises_and_budgets.md) | Does a bounded refusal mean that no reply can ever work? |
| [04. Price the next step](04_price_the_next_step.md) | Can a tie now affect the price later? |
| [05. Recheck a shortcut when the task changes](05_recheck_the_shortcut.md) | Why can a separation shortcut fail for matching? |
| [06. Carry the question list into reality](06_questions_and_reality.md) | What would an application to real objects still need? |

Work through the static figures and tables in order. Pause at each
prediction before reading the explanation. Then try the changed table in
[practice](PRACTICE.md); its [answers](ANSWERS.md) are separate.

This is a selected introduction to `research/identification_dual`.
Its [source map](SOURCE_MAP.md) distinguishes supplied rules, finite
calculations, and open claims. [Development notes](DEVELOPMENT.md)
explain how to reproduce the calculations.

AuthorityEffect: None. The research adds a requirement for equal profiles;
it adds no operation that merges internal identities. These examples are
bounded computations, not general Lean theorems or measurements of nature.
