# Practice: explain what an envelope log establishes

[Reasoning lab](README.md) · [Answers after your attempt](EVIDENCE_ANSWERS.md)

All rows below are **supplied fiction**. Use different words from the
lessons when you can, and give a reason rather than just a status label.

A proposed application says: “On envelopes A, B and C, inspected on the
front under procedure E1, if a star is visible, the flap is sealed.”
E1 records Y, N or ?. The two features are inspected separately.

| Trial | Envelope | Procedure/view | Star | Sealed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a1 | A | E1/front | Y | Y |
| b1 | B | E1/front | Y | N |
| b2 | B | E1/front | Y | Y |
| c1 | C | E1/front | N | ? |
| c2 | C | E1/front | ? | Y |
| c3 | C | E1/front | Y | ? |
| d1 | D | E1/front | Y | N |
| a2 | A | E2/back | Y | N |

1. Which row conflicts with the scoped conditional under its stated
   interpretation? Why do a1 and b2 not erase it?
2. Explain the different statuses of c1, c2 and c3 without replacing ?.
3. Explain why d1 and a2 do not refute the narrower E1/front claim on A/B/C.
   Does that make their records useless or justify deleting them?
4. Could a deterministic table indexed only by envelope assign both
   b1 and b2's clear sealed answers? What would adding trial identity
   change, and what would it not explain?
5. Classify these sentences by their jobs: defining the Y legend;
   deriving a contradiction from a conditional plus Yes/No;
   counting the supplied rows; reporting an inspection actually performed.
   Does classifying a report authenticate it?
6. Give two possible application failures behind a mismatch and an
   additional check for each. Explain why neither is established yet.
7. Describe one fresh prediction and a protocol for checking it. Keep
   invented expectations separate from observations you would actually record.

Record where an unfamiliar word blocked you. These tasks supply no
novice comprehension or transfer result until a reader attempts them.
