1. **b1** is in scope and supplies star Yes / sealed No. The universal
   conditional conflicts with that interpreted row. Compatible rows
   do not remove an individual counterexample.
2. **c1 is compatible:** its first condition is No. **c2 is compatible:**
   its required second answer is Yes. **c3 is unresolved:** either a
   sealed Yes or No can complete its unknown answer, with different outcomes.
3. D is outside the declared envelope list. E2/back differs from E1/front.
   Retain and classify these records; another claim may address them.
   Do not silently change the scope of this claim to use or exclude a row.
4. When the envelope alone is the input to a deterministic reading model,
   it cannot receive both clear outputs. Trial identifiers
   make the inputs different. That repairs this formal function issue
   without explaining physical variability, procedure changes or errors.
5. The jobs are definition, deduction, finite computation and measurement
   report. A report still needs its actual source and conditions. The
   sentences and rows in this exercise remain supplied fiction.
6. For example, an incorrectly labeled front/back reading and an
   accurate in-scope counterexample are different possibilities. Check
   the view/label record for the first; obtain a fresh separately recorded
   in-scope inspection for the second. These checks are proposals, not
   a verdict identifying which explanation holds.
7. A useful answer fixes the domain, procedure, legend and conditions
   before the new reading, states what would disagree with the prediction,
   preserves raw rows and uncertainty, and limits the conclusion to the
   cases and assumptions actually tested. No particular reading is required.
