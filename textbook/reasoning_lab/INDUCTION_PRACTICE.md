# Induction practice: check the start and the step

Attempt these before the [answer guide](INDUCTION_ANSWERS.md). Explain
why a premise holds or fails, rather than only giving a number.

1. A tray starts with four counters and each update adds three. Find
   the total after three updates. State the start and step cases for
   the formula 4 + 3 × n.
2. A tray starts with one counter and adds two each time. Someone claims
   its total is 2 × n after n updates. Which proof job fails?
3. A rule returns 2 × n through n=3, then 2 × n + 1. List its first five
   values. Do its first four matching values establish the packet rule
   for every count? Identify a failed next-step equation.
4. Begin with a ledger containing an admitted datum and a form. Replay
   a history of supplies of different tokens p and q. Does the proof
   require an empty starting ledger? Explain the empty-list case.
5. Replace one supply by token-erased Lean `admitDatum`. Can the
   all-supplies proof still be applied? What changes? Would Python's
   repeated-token admission be the same event definition?
6. A strictly increasing nonnegative whole-number measure is capped by
   two. How many recorded states and rounds does the general bound
   allow? What changes if a round repeats measure two?
7. A device matches the packet model on five trials. Does the model's
   induction proof establish the device's output on every later trial?
   Name a correspondence or update premise that still needs evidence.
