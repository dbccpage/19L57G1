rule, a record, a definition, a function, a one-way conditional, its

first condition. Its second comparison distinguishes pair-dependent
witnesses from a common witness. Its recovery detail shows blocking
pairs or all Boolean lookup functions on the selected domain. Empty
card and question domains remain distinct.

are not derived from the opaque mark. The source map connects the
applications to native contexts/separation and the actual compression
