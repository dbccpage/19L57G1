# A reading path with places to stop

You do not need to finish every laboratory before beginning the book.
Start with the core route below. The later applications are branches
to return to when their question interests you.

## First: names, answers and one blocked lookup

Read [First steps, Lessons 00–04](first_steps/README.md), using the three
cards beside the explanations. Then attempt the
[new-example reader check](first_steps/READER_CHECK.md) before its
[answer guide](first_steps/READER_CHECK_ANSWERS.md).

Pause here and explain two things in your own words: which question
separates a chosen pair, and which pair blocks a requested lookup.
If either answer is unclear, revisit First steps 01 or 04. A new name
alone does not give the machine another observation.

## Next: read what a claim asks

Read [Reasoning Lessons 00–07](reasoning_lab/00_rule_and_record.md), ending
with [matching evidence to a claim](reasoning_lab/07_evidence_for_a_claim.md).
Attempt the [packet practice](reasoning_lab/PRACTICE.md), then use the
[separate answers](reasoning_lab/ANSWERS.md) to inspect your reasons.

Before moving on, give a row that breaks an if-then claim, explain why
its reverse is another claim, and retain the domain of a lookup. If
needed, revisit Reasoning 02, 03 or 06 respectively. You can stop at
this point with a useful toolkit; the instrument and chance models are
later applications of it.

## Follow one demand through an actual model

Continue to the [Search lab](search_lab/README.md), then the
[Change lab](change_lab/README.md). Each starts a separately supplied
model. Keep a proposed repair, its counted price and an executed update
distinct. Try each route's changed practice before its answer guide.

For the formal source development, use
[Reading a formal statement](reading_formal_statements.md), then
[Unit 00](units/00_mark_and_constructor.md). The [textbook hub](README.md)
keeps Units 00–74 in source order. You can return to a concrete lab when
a definition needs an example; the research branches are not admission
requirements for starting Unit 00.

The [Reasoning hub](reasoning_lab/README.md) keeps all branches available.
Each supplies its model explicitly. Sharing words such as “state,”
“profile” or “observer” does not make the models interchangeable.

There is no promised reading speed or score here. Use a prediction, a
changed example and your explanation to decide where to pause. Software
checks support the calculations; understanding and retention need reader
evidence. An example of a device claim still needs a tested correspondence
between the model and the device.

AuthorityEffect: None. Reading guidance and proposed prerequisites, not a measured learning outcome.
