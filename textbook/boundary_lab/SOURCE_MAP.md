## Deliberately limited treatment

This route introduces selected results in three trees. It does not replay
the complete boundary-seed challenge campaign, revalidate all side-free
custody/comparison gates, reproduce a reported blind-run synthesis, or
settle the wider U/O price comparison. In particular, it does not turn
the control's scoped cost tie into “the boundary bought nothing.”
Other routes and the ledgers remain necessary for a fuller treatment.

The drawn coordinates and colors are teaching choices. The division here
is not the Path lab's signed boundary operation. The interactive desk
reads finite recorded cases rather than learning from physical observations.
Comprehension, transfer, reading time, and retention remain unmeasured.
