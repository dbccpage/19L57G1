# Boundary lab: what does a swap reveal?

Two records carry the same label. We can exchange their positions without
changing that label. Have we found two objects that are the same, two that
our record fails to distinguish, or two that a rule forbids us to exchange?
Those are different claims.

Begin after the [Reasoning lab](../reasoning_lab/README.md) and
[Map lab](../map_lab/README.md). [Unit 37](../units/37_an_inventory_is_not_a_derivation.md)
supplies a useful optional introduction to two-dot arrow pictures.

This route enters three research models: `boundary_seed`, `side_free`,
and `oriented_boundary`. They have different declarations. A shared word
does not make their theorems interchangeable. Here a *boundary* is an
admitted two-sided division, not the signed boundary operation of the
Path lab. No bridge identifying the two is asserted.
