# What remains before the textbook is finished?

AuthorityEffect: None. Status review: 2026-11-01 UTC.

The textbook has substantial content: 75 derivation Units, 18 institution
Units, ten beginner routes, worked examples, practice and twelve overview
videos. All 93 formal Units have a First pass opening. This is an existing
book undergoing correction and teaching review, not an unwritten book.

It is not ready for an unqualified completion claim. The remaining work
needs a finite review plan, not an indefinite stream of new laboratories
or repeated polishing of already checked pages. No percentage or reading
speed is established by the current evidence.

## Completion requirements and current evidence

| Requirement from the goal | What exists | What still needs closure |
|---|---|---|
| Correct grammar, definitions and mathematical scope | All Unit sources exist; dated reviews identify corrections and their source proofs | Finish the claim-by-claim reconciliation of the retained teaching claims and their prerequisites; source placement alone is insufficient |
| A usable route from no assumed background | The reading path starts with cards, rules and lookup examples, then branches to the tools needed later | Walk the chosen core route end to end with its stated prerequisites; resolve unexplained terms and jumps, rather than adding every branch to the required entrance |
| Definitions in manageable steps | All 93 Units have concrete openings; selected Units separate optional formal detail | Finish checking density, non-examples and changed practice where an opening still jumps to several unfamiliar ideas at once |
| Exact source custody and reproducible examples | Complete Unit text is checked against its pinned source; Python/Lean checks cover stated model examples | Bind the final release to its actual source and published pages; label selected checks separately from the repository merge contract |
| Useful treatment of the sealed development and research | All 75 sealed modules have source-placement entries; 18 of 123 research trees have selected introductions | Complete the placement decisions: teach, optional reference, open question, or justified omission. A reference is not teaching, and an introduction is not complete coverage |
| Readable videos and pages | Twelve corrected video pairs have a dated render/layout review; named desktop and phone samples are checked | Preserve the checked assets and inspect any changed media; extend page checks where new layouts introduce a concrete concern |
| Clear reasoning about reality | Lessons distinguish model answers, hypotheses, predictions, protocols and physical observations | Keep those boundaries in every retained application; no missing entry, search receipt or theorem alone establishes a physical observation |
| Reader comprehension | The author reported reading the material and finding it acceptable; separate changed-example questions and answers exist | Use that feedback without claiming measured results for other readers. No independent timed or delayed-learning result is recorded |

The last row does not reopen a request for a participant. The author has
already responded. A future observed learner trial could support a stronger
outcome claim; it is separate from finishing the text and documenting the
author's acceptance. Software checks do not measure intuition or retention.

## Finish the book in this order

1. **Close the current repair review.** Units 17–22 distinguish accepted
   requests, successful replay, all-request validity, minimum cost and
   renaming. Correct the reading-F data count and prove the missing
   general cost-floor arguments. Publish the checked pages and practice.
2. **Make one remaining-claims list.** Reconcile the retained claims in
   Units 23–74 and Institutions 00–17 against the dated reviews, sources
   and prerequisite route. Reuse completed review evidence. Reopen a
   checked claim when wording, source or a discovered defect warrants it.
3. **Close research placement decisions.** Review the existing inventory
   by teaching purpose. Give a dedicated lesson only when it serves the
   route; classify tooling, historical experiments, duplicate developments
   and open bridges as references or justified omissions where appropriate.
4. **Perform the release reading and publication check.** Follow the core
   route and its changed practice, fix actual breaks, verify the final
   source and selected public artifacts, and state the remaining open
   mathematics as open. The author controls merging.

These are ordered work packages, not completion receipts. The full goal
stays open while retained claims, prerequisite jumps or placement decisions
remain unresolved. Adding all 105 other research trees as new chapters is
not an implied requirement and would obscure the beginner route.
