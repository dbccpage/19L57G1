# Growth lab: why can a settled task reopen?

Begin after the [Change lab](../change_lab/README.md) and
[Map lab](../map_lab/README.md). We now enter research outside the sealed
derivation: `standing_commitment`. Its rules are supplied, and its own
source distinguishes proved statements from finite accounting experiments.

Imagine keeping records of items arriving one at a time. A standing rule
says which pairs must remain distinguishable. A new arrival can create
work without anyone writing a separate rule for that particular pair.
What has been supplied, and what actually follows from it?

For a concrete application to investigate, imagine a lost-property desk
recording only “blue?” and “bag?”. Its records distinguish all the items
currently held. A new blue bag can share an old item's answers. If the
desk must distinguish the items to return them correctly, the old record
may no longer be enough. The requirement comes from the job; it is not
created by the color question. The lab makes that difference precise.
