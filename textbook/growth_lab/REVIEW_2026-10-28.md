## What the reader can do

Eight short lessons teach pair listing, a standing rule, observations,
unresolved requirements, conditional generation, reopening, declared
service, and a refuted budget regularity. The route introduces parity
and membership notation in words before using them. Each lesson asks for
a prediction and ends with a small explanation task. Separate practice
uses new item labels, views, and a supplied arrival schedule.

The reader sees two distinct models. The represented-world model changes
only its held list during growth and reads demands from commitments plus
views. The source's race experiment assumes service removes queue entries;
it constructs no observation repair. A quiet represented state can reopen
only under the stated hypotheses. An end-clear service run can have an
earlier backlog. A longer horizon can let a bounded commitment catch up.
