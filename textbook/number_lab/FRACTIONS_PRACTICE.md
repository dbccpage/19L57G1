# Fraction practice with changed pieces

Try these after Lessons 09–12, before the separate
[answers](FRACTIONS_ANSWERS.md). Keep the whole and the unit explicit.

1. Is 6/8 the same value as 3/4? Does that make 1/4 the same as 1/8?
2. Add 1/3 and 1/4, showing a common piece size. Explain why adding
   numerator and denominator separately would answer another question.
3. Take 2/3 of 9/10 of a sheet. Give the product before and after reducing.
4. How many half-sheet units fit into 3/4 of a sheet? Explain why a result
   greater than one is appropriate here and check it by multiplication.
5. Which operations are supplied: 0 ÷ (1/4), (1/4) ÷ 0, and 3/0?
   Distinguish a zero numerator, zero divisor and zero denominator.
6. Compare `(1/2 + 1/4) × 2/3` with `1/2 + (1/4 × 2/3)`.
   Write each intermediate result; do not move the brackets silently.
