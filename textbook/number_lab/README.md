# Number lab: from counts to a combined reading

Two separate numerical records can agree while a combined reading differs.
To understand why, we will build the arithmetic a step at a time: totals,
shares, signed steps, squares, and pairs of coordinates.

For arithmetic, begin after [First steps](../first_steps/README.md) and
[core Reasoning 00–07](../reasoning_lab/00_rule_and_record.md). Read
Number 00–02, then the fraction toolkit 09–12 below. That branch needs
no prior map theory. Lesson numbers retain the earlier publication order.

The numbers and operations are supplied mathematical tools, not
consequences of the opaque mark. The [reading path](../learning_path.md)
shows where each later numerical application belongs.
