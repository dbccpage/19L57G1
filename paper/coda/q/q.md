# q

**[declared metatheory]** Reference (read-only, not imported):
`008_coda_q.md`. This page is a skeleton of the subject. Nothing here
is a theorem, a receipt, or an admitted claim.

Some questions are too large, volatile, or loaded to treat as ordinary
claims: what a quantum representation is, what consciousness is, and
other probes of that size. This layer is a holding rule, not a place
that answers them.

A probe names a bounded question, tries a finite descent, and publishes
exactly where the attempt fails. If there is no terminal record, the
question is not promoted.

A representation of a system is not the system. A Hilbert-space vector
is a way of organizing engagements; it is not thereby the furniture of
the world. This layer does not construct standing, and it does not
settle uniqueness claims about the continuum, Born rule, or Hilbert
space.
