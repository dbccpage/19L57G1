# foc

**[declared metatheory]** The bind of this layer is `coda/foc/paper.md`.
This reserved page is not that bind. External frame (read-only, not
imported): `000_coda_foc.md`.

A finite packet is a short chain of real vector spaces, a repair map at
the first step, a map to the next defect, and a second repair map there.
The first two maps compose to zero. Coordinates carry strictly positive
weights.

A leftover is the weighted L1 distance from a vector to the image of its
repair map. The same construction is applied to the image of the next
defect. Both distances are attained. A dual problem, over functionals
that kill the whole repair image and stay inside the weights, matches
the distance.

Those two numbers sort every valid packet: repaired at the first step;
not repaired there but repaired after the next map; or leftover at both
steps. Direct sums add the numbers. A compatible change of spaces can
stretch them only by a stated factor. Changing the weights can change
the number and the nearest representative; it cannot turn a zero class
into a nonzero one, or the reverse.

A diagnosis is not a declaration that the leftover has been closed.
