# mur

**[declared metatheory]** Reference (read-only, not imported):
`002_coda_mur.md`. This page is a skeleton of the subject. Nothing here
is a theorem, a receipt, or an admitted claim.

Rewriting an object — compiling it, coarsening it, translating it,
forgetting part of it — does not keep whatever standing it had just
because the new writing is related to the old.

A move from one writing to another needs three things: a check that the
target's descent data matches the image of the source's, an explicit
list of what the move dropped, and an actual stop in the target. A
certificate of the move is not enough on its own.

A lossy map must say what it lost. Composing two moves pastes their
certificates and adds their listed losses. Changing the writing is not
the same as changing what the thing is.
