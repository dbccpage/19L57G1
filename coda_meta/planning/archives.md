# Archives — hashed history, not a build input

**AuthorityEffect:** None. Plan only. Do not ingest, rewrite,
or re-home an archive in this phase.

## What they are

Two dumps are the best history on hand:

- the **Omega Engine archive**
- the **Anomalon archive**

They answer: who we were, where this came from, and whether a
good idea was forgotten. They do not answer: what to compile
tomorrow.

They are not first remotes. They are not OEC. They are not
this vault. They do not sit on the producer/consumer path in
`repo_split.md`.

## What happens to them

They stay where they are. An operator pass will hash them and
clean what is safe to clean. That pass is outside this
repository, on a separate paid session, and is **not** a gate
here. Nothing in CI waits on it. Nothing in the four products
imports from an archive until a later, named selection names
a hashed object and a reason.

A hash is a receipt that a tree was seen. It is not a claim
that the payload is true, minimal, or still wanted.

## What must not happen

- Do not migrate archive contents into `19L57G1` to "have
  everything in one place." This vault cites. It does not
  become the attic.
- Do not treat an archive as `main` of OEC or OEAS. Live
  products start clean and **pull selected history** if a
  later review names it.
- Do not assign bit keys to archive papers from this folder.
  Only `0000` is assigned (`open_conflicts.md` §5).
- Do not confuse these dumps with
  `C:\Users\a343o\source\repos\anomalon`, which
  `multi_repo.md` already records as an abandoned
  dual-language mine and not a live prefix.

## Prefix

`multi_repo.md` already reserved **OEE** as estate archive
vocabulary. That letter may later name history objects. It is
not a fourth-plus-one product remote. A second archive dump
does not automatically earn a second prefix; two dumps can
live under one history letter until a review says otherwise.
