## 1. Still a presentation

The measurement layer is not a rock under the other writings. It is
another presentation: a declared license, a declared gauge, a number.

Change the license or the gauge and the number can change. The zero
test — whether anything sticks — is the part that does not flip when
the positive weights are swapped. Ranking two nonzero leftovers can
flip. That is why a fluent report of "the residual is X" has already
chosen a spike.

A diagnosis is not a grant that the leftover has been closed. Closing
it, or treating it as granted, is the next layer.

## 2. The paper

A finite packet is:

- finite real spaces and maps `d0`, `d1` with `d1 d0 = 0` as an
  **operator** identity, not as a per-instance inheritance
- a declared offset `v` and stage-1 license `A1 = v + image(d0)`
  (`v = 0` recovers the linear-through-origin special case)
- compatibility `d1 v` lies in the stage-2 repair image (otherwise the
  three-way sort is not total)
- strictly positive coordinate weights

The leftover `Phi` is the weighted L1 distance from a vector to `A1`.
The next-defect leftover `Gamma` is the same construction after `d1`.
Both distances are attained on a finite packet. A dual functional that
kills the whole repair image and stays inside the weights matches the
distance.

Those two numbers sort a valid packet:

1. on the license (`Phi = 0`)
2. off the license, but the next defect is repairable (`Phi > 0`,
   `Gamma = 0`)
3. leftover at both steps

The offset is declared, the same way the weights are declared. Picking
a preferred origin is how the published linear-only packet threw away
the first paper's objects (trace-one states, retract images that are
not subspaces through the origin).

This is the paper: what sticks after every allowed repair.

## 3. Spikes are choices

Anything you attach after the leftover is a spike. Taking a spike does
not enlarge the paper. It does not authorize the spike. It does not
derive the host you climbed into.

**Born, as already written.** The note `qm_020` is this spike, named
and fenced.

Given nonnegative additive sector weights, not all zero, the unit-sum
law

`P(gamma) = w_gamma / sum w`

is already there. No Hilbert space is required for that line. An
optional lift `c_gamma = sqrt(w_gamma) e^{i theta}` reproduces the
**same** law through the Born formula. Normalization is a redundant
encoding of weights you already had.

Born *uniqueness* — that the quadratic form is the only reading — is
not forced by L1 additivity. That uniqueness is an imported packet
(Hilbert, projections, Gleason-type assumptions). The paper that
proves the cheap identity is also the paper that fences the expensive
one as `OracleNeeded`.

So: you can take Born normalization from L1 weights. That does not
mean you derived quantum mechanics. To get the quantum mechanics
people already know, you have to go up to Hilbert space and prove how
you got there. This layer does not do that work. It does not care to.

**The quantum series, sorted.** From `qm_000` to the loop-quantum-gravity
shadows (`qm_110`) the climb is mostly off this paper. The real-graph
half of `qm_000`, the leftover-versus-object split (`qm_002`), and the
L1 half of residual density are this paper. Born, diagonal density, and
a channel's stochastic shadow are spikes (`qm_012`, `qm_020`, `qm_082`).
Interference (`qm_061`) is the first thing L1 cannot compute. Hilbert
uniqueness, Schrödinger, Dirac, and `qm_110` are priced hosts or
shadows. Their own ingredient ladder already says rungs 1–5 are
bookkeeping and rung 6 is the surplus.

**Regge toward the standard model.** The general-relativity toy
(`constraint_closure_l1_toy_model.md`) is another climb: primitive
difference through a Regge mesh toward gauge, Higgs, alpha, SUSY,
dark sector. Track A writes the motifs. Track B is the kill: mostly
red. Constraint-closure toys generate candidates. They do not
terminalize physics. Class A (finite defects, additive L1, exact loop
closure) is this paper. Class C (standard-model derivation, physical
charges, continuum gravity) is not. The month of code and its own
diagnostics are the receipt.

Other spikes (priced, not taken here): a continuum window; a dual
circle after a declared free period; an infinite discrete host. Each
is a choice. Each must show its import. None is the paper.

## 4. What this layer does not do

- It does not mint standing. Fluency, a green test, a dual witness,
  and a small leftover are not a grant.
- It does not construct Hilbert space, unitary dynamics, or the Born
  rule as ontology.
- It does not walk a Regge mesh to the standard model, to SUSY, or to
  a physical cosmological constant. That climb is a toy with kill
  tests, not this paper.
- It does not settle Cantor, the Riemann hypothesis, or any continuum
  question. Those may descend to a finite packet. They may not.
- It does not decide which presentations transport or compress. That
  is a later layer.
- It does not authorize itself.
