## What this page is

This is the finished statement of the license layer, as the author
holds it. It is not a second copy of the published general-theory
monograph. It is not a cosmic authorizer. It is not the leftover.

The measurement layer says what sticks. This layer says whether anyone
is allowed to treat a candidate as granted, and where the check is
allowed to stop.

Most of the system's rule sheet is already here: no layer authorizes
its own output.

External frames (read-only, not imported): the published finite-descent
gate paper; its nonclaim list; the five-field packet and seven-guard
toy in that package's code; the observer / observation dual; the
flypaper bind (`coda/foc/paper.md`). The sealed derivation is evidence
that evaluation does not pick a unique answer. Cite it. Do not revise
it.

## 1. Two acts, not one

A leftover is a number under a declared license and gauge. Standing is
a separate act: a finite record that a candidate may count as granted
**inside a declared finite system**.

The published constructor (external frame) writes that as: a five-field
packet, a path that actually reaches a stop, and a halt witness
derived from evidence, not from a free true/false. Fluency, a logit,
a benchmark, and a small leftover do not inhabit that type.

This page keeps the split and drops the tower costume.

- **Evaluation** compares, under a declared observer packet.
- **Authorization** admits a change, or refuses it.

The observer packet makes a claim evaluable. It does not make it
licenseable. An accepting observation is not a grant. A residual
terminal is not standing.

---

## 2. The record

Standing, when it exists, is a finite record:

1. **What** is being judged (the carrier).
2. **Who evaluates** (the declared evaluator class).
3. **Who checks** (a verifier that is not the proposer).
4. **Where the check may stop** (the terminalizer).
5. **Under which rules** (the discipline).

The published names are `K`, `E`, `V`, `tau`, `Lambda`. The words
matter more than the letters. If any field is empty, there is no
packet. No packet is a refusal, not a silent pass.

The path must actually reach the stop. A pipeline that never
terminalizes has no standing. A pipeline that terminalizes in a
refusal has empty standing.

The halt witness is a conjunction derived from evidence the host
actually ran. The published toy lists seven: accepted; deterministic
replay; leftover mass at the declared zero; skeleton preserved;
declared candidates exhausted; no pending lift; no active refusal.
Callers may not hand the gate a free boolean named "authorized."

That toy's leftover-mass number is **not** the flypaper `Phi`. The
package says so. Do not cite one as the other.

## 3. What does not create standing

- The proposer. A language model, a solver, a climb, a fluent
  paragraph.
- A score, a confidence, a benchmark, a green test.
- A leftover of zero, a dual witness, a three-way diagnosis.
- An observation that accepted under some evaluator.
- Membership in a continuum story, a quantum host, or a Regge mesh.
- This page. Writing the rule is not inhabiting the type.

If the record is missing, the answer is a refusal. If the check ends
in a refusal, standing is empty.

Not every true statement is required to have such a record. Difference
does not need this layer in order to exist. This layer does not invent
standing. It only says when a candidate is allowed to count as granted
inside a declared finite system.

---

## 4. No layer authorizes its own output

That is the whole rule sheet, layer by layer.

The measurement presentation does not close itself by printing `Phi`.
The Born spike does not become quantum mechanics by reproducing a
unit-sum law. The Regge climb does not become the standard model by
generating motifs. Track A may be written and loved. Track B is the
kill. Candidates are not grants.

Transport, comparison, and continuum windows inherit the same stop.
A writing that moves well is not thereby licensed. Several writings
side by side are not a better world.

The wrap that listed every rule is mostly this page restated at each
layer. Do not grow a second constructor to say it again.

---

## 5. What this layer does not do

- It does not claim every truth admits a finite packet.
- It does not claim verification is decidable on an unbounded carrier.
- It does not identify standing (a type) with authorization (a
  process), or either with "authority" as a cosmic noun.
- It does not identify the observer with the halt witness.
- It does not claim this is the only permission kind in the estate
  (transport, submit, and act remain later, if they earn their own
  records).
- It does not mint `AuthorityEffect`. This package's effect is none.
