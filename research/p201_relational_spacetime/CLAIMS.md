# Claims Ledger: Target P201 / P202 (Relational Dimensions, Calibration & Causal Relational Clocks)

## 1. What it supports

1. **Relational Quantities and Unit Covariance**:
   Demonstrates in this discrete formal setting that physical quantities can be represented relationally with respect to calibration standards, transforming covariantly ($Q(x; \lambda U) = \lambda^{-1} Q(x; U)$) under standard rescaling.
2. **Dimensionless Ratio Invariance**:
   Shows that cross-ratio comparisons between quantities sharing a common dimension are strictly invariant under unit standard rescalings.
3. **Cross-Dimensional Separation & Refusal**:
   Demonstrates that distinct physical dimensions (Length, Duration, Count, Action) are disjoint and cross-dimensional operations return an explicit refusal, preventing illegal scalarization.
4. **Future-Test Congruence**:
   Shows that future-test state equivalence ($\forall k \ge 0, \pi(\mathrm{step}^k(s_1)) = \pi(\mathrm{step}^k(s_2))$) is a strict equivalence relation and a congruence with respect to execution steps ($s_1 \sim_F s_2 \implies \mathrm{step}(s_1) \sim_F \mathrm{step}(s_2)$).
5. **Relational Clock Synchronization**:
   Demonstrates that embedded internal monotonic counters act as relational clocks whose relative progression rate is an exact rational constant in $\mathbb{Q}$, independent of external host scheduling.

## 2. What it does not support

1. Does not support continuous 4-dimensional pseudo-Riemannian manifolds or general relativity field equations ($G_{\mu\nu} = 8\pi T_{\mu\nu}$); all carrier geometry is strictly discrete and relational.
2. Does not claim a derivation of quantum time-energy uncertainty relations.
3. Does not claim continuous-variable differential calculus.

## 3. The narrowest defensible claim

Under the declared discrete relational state and clock model, quantities transform covariantly under unit rescaling, dimensionless ratios are gauge-invariant, cross-dimensional operations refuse cleanly, future-test equivalence is a step-congruence, and internal clock gear ratios are exact in $\mathbb{Q}$.
