## The question

Read the sealed estate's first standing obstruction the other way.

The obstruction records what was not derived.  This rung asks the
complementary question, which is about the grammar rather than about the
derivation: in the sealed grammar extended by a declared two-sided stake,
where can a demand come from at all?

Prove, over a verbatim reconstruction of the sealed kernel and of the
pinned one-sided stake:

- that a supply changes no state, and therefore changes no demand;
- that with no stake declared -- equivalently, under the stake that
  labels every pair free -- the demand set is empty at every regime
  whatever, reached by any sequence of sealed events or not reached at
  all, because a demand is read off the stake and the profiles and off
  nothing else;
- that every event kind of the sealed grammar is one of the six the
  rung-zero census recorded, each carrying natural-number fields only,
  so that no event of the grammar introduces a stake;
- that whether a pair is a demand depends on the stake only through the
  label the declaration gives that pair;
- and that with a stake declared, growth generates: the one-sided form
  is the pinned theorem, reconstructed with its hypotheses unchanged,
  and the two-sided form over the sealed regime is proved here, in both
  label directions.

Conclude, with the scope in the same sentence: in this grammar there is
no first demand from inside; demands propagate from a declaration and
never start; the declaration is not an event of the object language.
The scope excludes any grammar that is not this one.

## Discipline

Every substantive line carries exactly one evidence class.  A target
true by unfolding a definition is recorded as being at the level of a
definition, in those words, and is never counted as content.  No theorem
is stated without its grammar in the same sentence.  Predictions are
frozen before the first computation and before the first compile, scored
mechanically, and never edited.  A receipt is not a proof.  A pin proves
only that a source did not move.  A gate is proved by failing on the
defect it exists to catch, with the failure naming the injected object,
and by recovering when the injection is reversed.
