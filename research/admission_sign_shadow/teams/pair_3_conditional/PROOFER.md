## Proposed lane

pair, a character, operational nontriviality, a character-to-integer
calibration, an integer mixing map, and an explicit separation law.  It then
proves that the two induced integer pairs have equal coordinate squares and
different mixed readouts.

The required negative cases are a collapsed pair, unequal erased endpoints,
the trivial character, a flat mixing map, and an exactly-three history carrier
with no free involution.

The type firewall declares two unrelated types.  `ReportSection` is a right
inverse for a report map.  `NaturalChoice` selects one pair at every object and
commutes with every declared arrow.  Countermodels in both directions test
whether either bare structure determines the other.
