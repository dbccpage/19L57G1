## Attacks on the first proposal

1. A fresh two-constructor type with a constant erased endpoint does not by
   itself correspond to the frozen histories.  A source pin records custody;
   it does not prove a typed identification.
2. A record containing only a value restricted to `1` or `-1` is not yet a
   character.  The identity and composition laws must be fields or the claim
   must be weakened to a declared exchange value.
3. `OperationalNontrivial` must stay imported and should be connected to an
   actual separation of the two local labels.
4. A free involution is not a complete torsor receipt without an action law
   and a unique-transporter theorem.
5. Two exhibited relative labels do not prove that global negation is the only
   ambiguity.  A classification and a base-normalized uniqueness theorem are
   required.
6. One failing three-cycle does not support a general claim about larger
   families.  The exact three-element statement is that every involution has a
   fixed point, hence no free involution exists on that carrier.
