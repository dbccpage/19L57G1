## CL-P209B-04: Eastin-Knill Finite Quotient Obstruction

- **Statement**: Transversal Clifford operations on discrete state carriers generate a strictly finite quotient group of order $p(p^2 - 1)$ on single carriers. Because the quotient is finite and discrete, transversal operations fail to form a dense subgroup in continuous unitary Lie groups $\mathrm{SU}(d)$, leaving a strictly positive covering gap.

- **Claim Discipline**:
  1. *What it supports*: Demonstrates that any set of transversal Clifford gates induces a finite group action on Pauli state carriers, precluding dense approximation of $\mathrm{SU}(d)$ without non-transversal resources.
  2. *What it does not support*: Does not imply that dense covering is unreachable when non-transversal resources (such as auxiliary magic state injection) are admitted.
  3. *Narrowest defensible claim*: In any setting where operations are restricted to transversal Clifford generators preserving a stabilizer code, the induced logical group quotients to a finite subgroup of $\mathrm{Sp}(2n, \mathbb{F}_p)$, precluding continuous Lie group density.
  4. *Next test needed*: Evaluate noise thresholds for magic state distillation outside the transversal Clifford quotient.
