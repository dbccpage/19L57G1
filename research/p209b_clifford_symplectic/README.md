## Core Theorems & Implementations

### 1. Canonical Symplectic Form Preservation over F_p^2
On the 2D discrete phase_space $\mathbb{F}_p^2$, the canonical bilinear form is defined by:
$$\omega(u, v) = (u_x \cdot v_z - u_z \cdot v_x) \pmod p$$
For the modular arithmetic used by the Python examples, a direct determinant identity gives, for a $2 \times 2$ matrix $M$:
$$\omega(M u, M v) \equiv \det(M) \cdot \omega(u, v) \pmod p$$
The cited Lean development checks form preservation for six explicit matrices modulo 2. A general group isomorphism is not formalized here; the Python field examples use moduli 2 and 3.

### 2. Six explicit form-preserving matrices modulo 2
Modulo 2, the named matrices generated by two explicit matrices are:
$$H = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}, \quad S = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$$
This group has order 6 and consists of:
- **Identity**: $I = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$ (order 1)
- **Order-2 Reflections**: $H, S, HSH = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
- **Order-3 Rotations**: $SH = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}, HS = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$

The package establishes:
- All 6 matrices have determinant $1 \pmod 2$.
- All 6 matrices are mutually distinct and preserve the symplectic form.
- Python evaluates their action on the 3 non-zero vectors. The cited Lean theorem proves closure in the explicitly listed six-matrix predicate; it does not construct a permutation-group isomorphism.

### 3. Python enumeration of determinant-one matrices modulo 3
For modulus 3, Python enumerates 24 determinant-one matrices. The package does not prove a Clifford quotient at this modulus in Lean.
$$|\mathrm{SL}(2, \mathbb{F}_3)| = 3(3^2 - 1) = 24$$
Generated by $H_3 = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 2 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ and $S_3 = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$.
The order spectrum across the 24 elements is:
- 1 element of order 1 ($I$)
- 1 element of order 2 ($-I$)
- 8 elements of order 3
- 6 elements of order 4
- 8 elements of order 6

### 4. Generated-family closure
The Lean theorem with a historical obstruction name states only that products generated by the two listed matrices belong to the six-matrix predicate. There is no code space, transversal gate semantics, topology, or density theorem in this package. Any such application needs those structures and a proved interpretation of this finite family.
