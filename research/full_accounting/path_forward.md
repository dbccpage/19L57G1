## What is already a system (native)

One object language, two starting inscriptions, one control, one
kind-classification, two leftover inputs.

1. **Inertia.** `<>` generates nothing. Supply is not admission.
2. **Collapse.** Admission without judgment structure does not
   separate. Two-ness is a purchase.
3. **Tied next.** The first required separation costs two; `mu1` does
   not pick a unique child; `mu2` does, by a further declaration.
   Single-valued succession is not a fact.
4. **Orientation is a toll.** Two-ness does not yield it equivariantly.
   One asymmetric admitted witness breaks the swap. You cannot
   withdraw the swap with no witness ((B) empty).
5. **Container is optional.** `<|>` admits a boundary as a primitive.
   Side-free: that primitive bought **no price** the admission order
   could not buy, under the unnamed reading. Empty sides are not an
   internal fact.
6. **Order ≠ container.** Different existence conditions. Orientation
   is not succession. No clock entered the language.
7. **Demands.** No object-language generator (`OB-1`). A *declared*
   standing commitment plus *external* growth produces demands with
   no act between. Dual demand (`RequireSame`) is a different cost
   landscape.
8. **Two leftover declarations.** Stake and stream. The second seed
   has not got below two. Engine / run-forward / definability census
   (Challenges 3–5) unstarted.
9. **Difference / observer layer.** Obstruction = retained ∧
   observer-zero ∧ protected. Natural section iff fixed admissible
   pair. Kernel constitution interprets itself only relative to a
   fixed interpreter. Absolute self-interpretation OPEN.
10. **Progress is licensed.** Proposals do not self-execute. Refusal
    is a non-absorbing record. Both orientations of an extension
    family are retained.

That is more than a kernel. It is a **closed account of what cannot
be free**. A physical-looking theory that needs a container, an
arrow, a unique next state, or a self-starting engine must *pay* for
each of those here. That is the recovery: not a model of a named
outside theory, but a price list for the pieces such a theory uses.

The author’s withheld reading of the boundary seed (one primitive, two
capability levels, imported nouns refused as claims about nature) is
in `research/oriented_boundary/AUTHOR_READING.md`. The object language
stopped at the kind split and the empty (B). A functor onto a
specified target is a **later, separate obligation**, or it is

## Path 2 — new repository from a more complex guarded seed

The first seed was `<>` with *no* object-language operations, then
challenges. That is why the foundation is evidence. A more complex
seed is worth doing only if the extra structure is **priced in the
seed as admitted primitives**, not smuggled as ordinary meanings of
marks.

A guarded seed that would generate the *system* rather than
rediscover the price list:

1. Inscription with an optional boundary mark, and the U/O readings
   declared as two towers from line one (so `<|>` is not a later
   surprise).
2. Explicit list of **admitted primitives** and their capability
   prices: boundary, side-names, asymmetric witness (orientation),
   standing commitment (stake), growth/stream. Each may be refused
   by a control that deletes it (the side-free move, generalized).
3. Explicit **forbidden free gifts**: unique next, observer from
   nowhere, witnessless withdrawal of Aut, endogenous demand,
   succession-as-object, absolute self-interpretation.
4. The same blindness, replay, iso, dual measures, retain-all-ties,
   classification vocabulary, and stopping conditions.
5. A freeze-then-compare slot that is *empty until freeze*, so a
   later functor onto a specified target can be a separate repo or
   a sealed final section — never an input.

What this buys: Challenges 0–3 of `<>` and C0–C3 of `<|>` and
side-free become *part of the seed’s declared menu*, and the first
live question is the second seed’s Challenge 3 (the engine) instead
of months of re-proving that containers and arrows are not free.

What this costs: it is **not** a continuation of the blind
derivation from `<>`. The whole value of 19L57G1 is that those
prices were *found*. A new seed that writes the prices in on page
one is a different experiment: it can *use* the foundation; it
cannot claim to have derived it again. Cite 19L57G1 as a frozen
input, the way `research/second/` already cites the sealed estate.

Guardrails the new seed must copy or it will collapse into a target
syllabus:

- No outside names until a freeze.
- Predictions hashed before code.
- Tied minima retained.
- Every “complete system” claim tagged: derived / declared / bounded /
  OPEN.
- Isolation of research trees; no shared pin-guard.
- An injection record for every new gate.
