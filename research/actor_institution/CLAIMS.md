# Claims

AuthorityEffect: None.

Statuses follow the research-tree contract. PROVED_THEOREM: in Lean, axioms
within `propext` and `Quot.sound` (the report is `lean/ResearchActorInstitution/AxiomReport.lean`).
VERIFIED_FINITE: computed exhaustively over the stated bound and silent
outside it. CONDITIONAL: in Lean under a named admission. OPEN: neither
proved nor refuted. REFUTED: a witness against a stated conjecture, in Lean
where the row says so. A VERIFIED_FINITE row never upgrades a theorem, and a
passing test is never cited for a PROVED_THEOREM row. `Inst.`, `Chain.`,
`Policy.` and `Acquisition.` abbreviate the namespaces under
`ResearchActorInstitution`; TT-numbers refer to `research/two_theorems/CLAIMS.md`.
