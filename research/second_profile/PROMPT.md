## The objects

A HISTORY is a finite sequence of events of the six sealed kinds,
replayed from a BASE regime by the sealed step.  A history REACHES the
regime its replay returns.  An ADMITTED datum of a regime is an index
below its number of data.

A CONTEXT, its PLUGGING by a datum, DERIVABILITY, COLLAPSE and
SEPARABILITY are the sealed ones.  Two admitted data are COLLAPSED when
no well-formed context tells them apart by positive derivability, and
SEPARABLE when some well-formed context does; the sealed separability
carries its witness.

A regime HOLDS TWO when some two admitted data of it are separable.
This is the reading of the census row "two-ness" that this rung
declares; it names two data told apart from inside the regime, and says
nothing of how many data are held.

An OBSERVATIONAL CLASS of a regime is a class of its admitted data under
collapse.  A REPRESENTATIVE LIST of a regime is a list of admitted data,
any two of them at different positions separable, such that every
admitted datum is collapsed with some entry of the list.  The CLASS
COUNT of a regime, in the finite cross-check, is the number of distinct
sealed profiles among its admitted data.

Two regimes are ISOMORPHIC when the sealed isomorphism structure has an
inhabitant between them.  A CLASS OF REGIMES, in the finite cross-check,
is the set of regimes the sealed key procedure gives one key.

## The question

- *One.*  Does the sealed isomorphism carry separability and collapse
  between admitted data, forward and back?  With which hypotheses on the
  two regimes, if any?  Does it carry holding two, and representative
  lists?
- *Two.*  Is collapse, restricted to admitted data, reflexive, symmetric
  and transitive, so that the observational classes are classes?
- *Three.*  What must a regime hold before it holds two?  From the
  sealed empty regime, and from each of the two sealed reading regimes,
  what is the least length of a history reaching a regime that holds
  two, proved as a lower bound for every history and shown attained by
  an exhibited one?
- *Four.*  Which of the six event kinds keep separability of a pair of
  data, for every regime and every pair?  Which can end holding two, and
  by what exhibited regime and event?
- *Five.*  Over the frozen rectangle, for each base: how many sequences
  reach a regime that holds two, and at what least length; the class
  count of every regime reached, and its largest value; whether any
  class of regimes carries two values of holding two or of the class
  count; by which event kinds holding two is ended; and, for each event
  kind, which changes of the class count one event makes.
- *Six.*  For the two census rows, which of DEFINABLE, USEFUL
  REALIZATION, FORCED or none of these each is, at which stage, and on
  what evidence; and which of the above is proved for every history,
  which is a finite computation on the rectangle, and which stays open
  beyond it.

No answer to any of the six is stated here and none is expected.  Each is
closed by a proof, by an executable countermodel, or by an explicit OPEN
status.
