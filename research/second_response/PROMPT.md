## The objects

A HISTORY, a BASE, the regime a history REACHES, an ADMITTED datum, a
CONTEXT, its PLUGGING, DERIVABILITY, COLLAPSE and SEPARABILITY are the
sealed ones, as rung nine used them.  A regime HOLDS TWO when some two
admitted data are separable.

A form of a regime DISTINGUISHES two admitted data when some well-formed
context on that form tells them apart by positive derivability.  A form
is EXHAUSTED when it distinguishes no two admitted data.  This is this
rung's reading of the census row "exhaustion, as a form that
distinguishes nobody".

A DEMAND of a regime is a pair of admitted data that are collapsed, read
off the regime by the sealed procedure.  A RESPONSE to a demand under a
measure is a minimum response of the sealed responder.  The RESPONDED
regime is the regime the response's admission events reach from the
demanded regime.  A pair of admitted data of the demanded regime is
MOVED by a response when it is separable in one of the demanded and the
responded regimes and collapsed in the other.

ADJACENCY: two distinct pairs of admitted data are ADJACENT at a regime,
under a measure, when some response to some demand of that regime moves
both.  The ADJACENCY GRAPH of a regime has the pairs of its admitted data
as vertices and adjacency as its edges; the DISTANCE between two pairs is
the least number of edges on a path between them in that graph, where
one exists.  This is this rung's reading of "adjacency, as which pairs a
single response can change together".

REACH: the reach of a response to a demand is the set of admitted data of
the demanded regime, other than the two demanded, that belong to some
pair the response moves.  This is this rung's reading of "reach, as the
third-party effect of a response".

BRANCHING: the tie count of a demand under a measure is the number of
responses to it, and its tie-class count is the number of classes of the
regimes they reach once the demanded pair is imposed, under the sealed
key procedure, as the sealed engine quotients its branches.  This is
this rung's reading of "branching, as the tie count".

Two regimes are ISOMORPHIC when the sealed isomorphism structure has an
inhabitant between them.  A CLASS OF REGIMES, in the finite cross-check,
is the set of regimes the sealed key procedure gives one key.

## The question

- *One.*  Is exhaustion of a form carried by the sealed isomorphism,
  forward and back, and with which hypotheses?  How is it related to
  holding two?  Which forms are exhausted by construction?
- *Two.*  Which of the six event kinds can begin the exhaustion of a
  form, which can end it, and which keep it, for every regime; which
  change is exhibited by a regime and an event?
- *Three.*  Which of the six event kinds can move a pair of admitted
  data, and in which direction; which pairs can one instance admission
  move; and so, which pairs can one response move together, for every
  well-formed regime and every history of admission events?
- *Four.*  Over the frozen rectangle, for each base and each measure:
  how many regimes lie in and outside the responder's domain; how many
  demands there are, and how many are refused within the sealed bounds;
  at which cost each is answered; the tie counts and tie-class counts;
  the pairs moved, their direction, and whether any response moves a
  pair that neither of its instances mentions; the reach of every
  response; the adjacency graph of every regime, its edges and its
  distances; the exhausted forms of every regime and how each event kind
  changes their number; and whether any class of regimes carries two
  values of any of these.
- *Five.*  Does the sealed responder, which prunes its enumeration by
  lemmas proved for this demand kind, agree with the unpruned sealed
  enumeration and the sealed reference validity on a declared family?
- *Six.*  For the four census rows, which of DEFINABLE, USEFUL
  REALIZATION, FORCED or none of these each is, at which stage, and on
  what evidence, and what an additional declaration, if one is needed,
  costs; which of the above is proved for every regime or history, which
  is a finite computation on the rectangle, and which stays open beyond
  it.

No answer to any of the six is stated here and none is expected.  Each is
closed by a proof, by an executable countermodel, or by an explicit OPEN
status.

## What is reconstructed and what is native

The sealed kernel's regime, well-formedness, contexts, derivability,
collapse, separability, event kinds, step, replay and genesis, and the
sealed isomorphism structure, are reconstructed verbatim from the sealed
sources, as rungs three to nine did, with a pin note and a
declaration-by-declaration comparison with the pinned sources.  Nothing
sealed and no sibling package is imported by the Lean of this rung.
Where a statement of this rung repeats a statement an earlier rung
proved, it is proved again inside this package so that the package
stays self-contained, and the ledger says whose statement it is.  No
sibling source is copied into the Python of this rung: the sealed
kernel, the sealed profiles, the sealed responder and its candidate
space, the sealed key procedure, the sealed genesis readings and the
rectangle of rungs three and four are consumed read-only through their
public functions, pinned by blob.

The sealed augmentation and response machinery is NOT reconstructed in
Lean.  So no Lean statement of this rung is about a minimum, a tie count
or a cost; the Lean speaks of events and of histories of events, and the
response rows' numbers are finite computations.

## Discipline

Every substantive line carries exactly one evidence class.  A target
true by unfolding a definition or by kernel computation on concrete
regimes is recorded as being at the level of a definition, in those
words, and is never counted as content.  No theorem is stated without
its grammar in the same sentence.  A demand read off the state is kept
distinct from a requirement imposed by an event.  A response computed by
the sealed responder is a model proposal and is never admitted: the
responded regime is read, and no state of the rectangle is changed by
it.  Predictions are frozen before the first computation and before the
first compile, scored mechanically, and never edited.  A receipt is not
a proof.  A pin proves only that a source did not move.  A gate is
proved by failing on the defect it exists to catch, with the failure
naming the injected object, and by recovering when the injection is
reversed.  Nothing of the sealed estate's standing obstructions is
treated here as anything other than standing.
