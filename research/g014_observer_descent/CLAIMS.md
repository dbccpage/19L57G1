# Claims Ledger: Target G014 (Multi-Observer Descent & Natural Transport)

## 1. What it supports

1. **Sheaf-like Descent on Finite Coverages**:
   Demonstrates that for finite discrete multi-locus carrier states, a family of local observer records covering the locus universe stitches into a global carrier valuation if and only if pairwise compatibility holds on all overlapping sectors.
2. **Observation Preservation**:
   Shows that gluing compatible local records preserves every required local observation ($\pi_i(\mathrm{glue}((r_k))) = r_i$).
3. **Explicit Obstruction Witnesses**:
   Witnesses that whenever local observer records disagree on a shared locus ($\rho_{ij}(r_i) \ne \rho_{ji}(r_j)$), no global carrier state can exist that projects to both records simultaneously, giving an explicit mismatch witness.
4. **Natural Transport Commutation**:
   Demonstrates that under frame automorphisms (locus permutations), observation readout commutes with transport ($\pi_{\sigma(O)}(\sigma(S)) = \sigma(\pi_O(S))$).
5. **Symmetric Duplicate Observer Agreement**:
   Shows in this model that when a carrier state is invariant under an automorphism swapping symmetric loci, duplicate observers measuring those loci yield identical readouts.

## 2. What it does not support

1. Does not support infinite-dimensional topological sheaf theory or continuous manifolds; all carriers and observer coverages are strictly finite and discrete.
2. Does not show that arbitrary continuous manifolds emerge automatically from finite covers without additional geometric constraints.
3. Does not establish non-commuting observable algebras; all observations in this target commute on state readouts.

## 3. The narrowest defensible claim

Under the declared discrete multi-locus carrier setting, compatible local observation projections over finite covers admit a unique global glued reconstruction preserving all local records, and any incompatible overlap generates an explicit falsifying obstruction witness.
