## Open findings of this comparison

- **COMPARE-MAP-OPEN.** No map from the sealed data/forms/instances/rules
  and profile observation to boundary marks/addresses/permitted renamings
  preserves the compared tasks, accepted events, histories and costs. The
  numerical differences above therefore do not prove that one primitive
  saves a stated number of the other grammar's items. For an absent task,
  its corresponding full boundary price is OPEN with B still charged.
- **COMPARE-TOTAL-PRICE-OPEN.** The native display tally omits no boundary
  charge, but it is not a full dependency sum. Occurrence identity, reader
  widenings and generator declarations need their own common-unit account
  before any total-history saving can be computed. Declaring that account
  now would be new work, not a correction of the frozen result.
- **COMPARE-C2-ATTRIBUTION.** The frozen C2 receipt preserves its historical
  names attribution. The later frozen B3 ledger and price fields explicitly
  narrow the operative separation to withdrawal of side exchange; names
  and the presentation convention are not the witness. Read the dated
  correction with the old receipt. Nothing frozen is rewritten here.
- **COMPARE-DEPENDENCE-OPEN.** The hand-written boundary-dependence maps
  are classified interpretations. Neither their serialization nor this
  comparison establishes a total computational removal experiment.
- **COMPARE-OBSERVATION-OPEN.** The sealed truth function combines rule
  coverage and instance membership by an or, but the sealed Regime and its
  event history retain those fields separately. Loss of provenance in one
  projected truth value is not proof that the state conflates admission
  and observation, nor that no other repository view can distinguish them.
  This run does not prove an estate-wide absence claim or import an
  unreviewed sibling's observer into its frozen model.
- **COMPARE-NEXT-OPEN.** The supply frontier is not a continuation theorem.
  No outside-of-the-outermost-boundary object, internal limit, universal
  initial process or unique generator follows from the bounded tree.
- **COMPARE-Q15-OPEN.** Within native vocabulary the comparison exhibits
  scoped similarities of replay, nonuniqueness and loss of distinction.
  No typed identification proving that a familiar structure is definable
  at a first stage was frozen. Such an identification is OPEN; a useful
  interpretation would not establish that it is forced.

These are comparison findings only. No source, proof, prediction, receipt,
status page or ledger of the frozen run has been revised in response.
The terminal remains bounded continuation with exact frontier and explicit
obstructions, not fifteen accepted rounds or unbounded generation. The
dedicated freeze commit must remain an ancestor of any published comparison;
squashing the source, marker and comparison together would destroy that
history. The seed reserves merging to the author.
