# Challenge 0 — predictions, written and hashed before any code or proof

`AuthorityEffect: None`.  Each prediction carries a falsifier and a
FORCED / NOT FORCED label: FORCED means the writer expects it to follow
from the definitions the run has not yet written, and a hit on a FORCED
prediction is evidence of the order of the work and not of insight.  A
missed prediction is recorded next to its value in `STATUS.md` and never
edited here.  The sha256 of this file as frozen is recorded in
`STATUS.md` and pinned by the tests.

- C0-1 (collapse under U).  Under reading U, with no mark on either
  side, the two sides are observationally collapsed: whatever the object
  language can observe of a side, it observes equally of both.  FORCED,
  once "observe of a side" is defined as its contents.  Falsifier: a
  definition of side observation under which the empty sides differ
  without an admitted name.
- C0-2 (distinction under O is bought, not found).  Under reading O the
  two sides are distinguishable with nothing on them, and the
  distinction is DEFINITION-level: it is the admitted names and nothing
  else.  FORCED.  Falsifier: a proof that the O-distinction has content
  beyond the declaration.
- C0-3 (inertia).  No object-language transition follows from the
  genesis state under either reading: a supply leaves the state
  unchanged, and no admission is entailed by the state.  FORCED.
  Falsifier: a state change with no admission event in its history.
- C0-4 (emptiness is not a fact).  Under both readings, that a side is
  empty is not an internally available fact at genesis: no observation
  fires, and the absence of an observation is not converted into a
  judgment.  NOT FORCED: it depends on whether the run admits a negative
  observation, which the seed does not admit.  Falsifier: an internal
  judgment "this side is empty" derivable at genesis without a new
  admission.
- C0-5 (the reflection).  Under U the exchange of the two sides is an
  automorphism of the genesis state and of every state whose sides carry
  the same contents; under O it is not, because the names are admitted.
  FORCED for genesis; NOT FORCED for the general statement.  Falsifier:
  a U-state fixed by nothing, or an O-state whose reflection is an
  automorphism.
- C0-X (the extrapolation, scored by Challenge 1).  After `<a|>` is
  supplied and its mark admitted on a side, the two sides become
  observationally separable under U with no augmentation beyond the
  admission itself, and under O nothing becomes distinguishable that was
  not already.  NOT FORCED as a whole: the U half depends on the
  admission grammar Challenge 1 declares.  Falsifier: under U the sides
  remain collapsed after one admitted mark, or under O the admission
  adds a distinguishable object beyond the mark.
