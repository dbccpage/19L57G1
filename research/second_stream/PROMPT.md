# PROMPT: rung one, the stream as a response

`AuthorityEffect: None`.  This file is the rung's question.  It states no
expected value; every number in it is a bound or an index, spelled out.

## The question

Challenge one of `research/second/SEED.md`: under a declared bound of three
items and a declared class of validity rules, at least the inherited
no-regress rule, determine whether a fresh datum is ever part of a minimum
response to any demand at any state reached within bounds.  If it is, price
it and retain every tie.  If it never is, state the impossibility with its
exact bounds and say what would falsify it at the next bound.

## What is kept distinct

An item is a member of the sealed augmentation grammar: a datum, a form, a
uniform rule on a form, or an instance.  An item is not a datum, and the
bound of three items is not a bound on admitted data, on accepted rounds,
or on held pairs.

A fresh datum in a response is a datum item of a retained minimum
augmentation, admitted by the response's own admission event and never
supplied.  Growth is the supply and then the admission of one fresh opaque
datum at a state carrying no demand.  The two are different acts and carry
different tokens.  Supply, admission, and current-state designation are kept
distinct throughout, and a demand read off the state is kept distinct from a
demand emitted by an act.

## The declared rectangle

From both sealed genesis readings, each closed by one declaration of one of
the five named commitments of the sibling tree `two_sided_commitment`, and
under each of the two sealed cost measures: enumerate responses of at most
three items with form arity at most two, take at most fifteen accepted
rounds, and stop expanding past a pre-expansion node guard of two hundred
and fifty nodes.  The sibling's data bound of five governs growth alone and
does not bind a response; a response admitting data beyond it is recorded
with the count it reaches, and with the effect of that count on the
bijection budget.  This rung moves the item bound and holds every other
bound at the value the sibling declared.

## The declared enumeration

The sealed kernel declares its unpruned enumeration only to two items and
refuses beyond it, so the candidate space at three items is declared by this
rung and is not the sealed one.  It is generated over the same four item
kinds, restricted by the sealed admission conditions alone, and consumes the
sealed kernel read-only through its public functions, copying no source.  No
sealed pruning lemma is used: each was proved for a separation demand, and
the demands here are of two kinds.  Below three items the rung defers to the
sealed unpruned enumeration rather than to its own.  The declaration is
certified against an independently written wider construction at every item
count from zero to three, and by reproducing the sibling's committed trees
when this rung's own item bound is set to two.

## The declared budgets

A three-item enumeration is reached only where every response of fewer items
fails, so the rung counts what it does rather than assuming it did it, and
it bounds the one expansion the node guard does not reach.  It declares: at
most forty enumerations at three items in one run; at most one million
candidates examined in one enumeration; at most two thousand valid responses
gathered at one demanded pair; at most two hundred retained classes at one
node; and a repeated enumeration answered from the record of the one before
it whenever the state, the commitment and the demanded pair agree exactly.
Each bound carries its own terminal, naming the tree, the node and the
demanded pair at which it fired, and a node whose gathering is cut short
attaches nothing.

## The declared validity

A response to a demand is valid when the augmented state honours the
demanded pair and honours every committed pair among the previously held
data that the state honoured before the response.  Nothing else is required
of it; other open demands stay open, and pairs involving a fresh datum are
new demands rather than regress.  That is the inherited no-regress rule,
replayed from the sibling tree before it is used.  This rung declares one
further member of the class, which additionally requires that every
committed pair involving a datum the response itself introduces be honoured
after the response, whether the other member is a previously held datum or
another datum the same response introduces.  Where each member is run is
declared, and every recorded row names the member that produced it.

## The declared targets and quotient

Every demanded pair is a target.  The sibling's own question says "take one
representative per automorphism orbit of the demanded pairs"; its shipped
code takes every demanded pair and quotients no orbit away.  This rung
inherits the shipped rule and records the departure.  States merge only
under the sibling's repaired key, which pairs the sibling's own state key
with the exact indexed regime and the commitment name.  Presentation tokens
stay forgotten.  The implemented isomorphism is cross-checked against a
minimum over all bijection pairs.

## The census of availability

At every node reached, and independently of the item count at which the
minimum was found, record whether a valid response containing a fresh datum
exists within a bound of two items; and at every state where a three-item
enumeration is performed, record the same within the bound of three items.
A fresh datum's presence among the valid responses is thereby recorded
separately from its presence among the minima, and each census carries the
count of nodes it visited and the count of candidates it examined.

## What is reported when the answer is negative

The impossibility is stated with its exact bounds; every run truncated by
the round bound, the node guard, the bijection budget, the enumeration
budget, the candidate budget, the response budget or the class budget is
named individually with its truncation kind; and the observation at a bound
of four items that would falsify it is stated.
