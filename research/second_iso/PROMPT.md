# PROMPT: rung four -- the first-demand index up to the implemented isomorphism

`AuthorityEffect: None`.  This file is the rung's question.  It states no
expected value and names no expected terminal; every number in it is
spelled out.  It is frozen by hash before anything else in this rung is
written, and before any Lean is built.

## Where the question comes from

Rung three defined, over the sealed grammar extended by a declared
two-sided stake, the first count at which a demand stands along a
history, proved it unique, and exhibited two histories from one base
that reach one and the same regime with different first counts.  It
named, as leftover X-3, priced small and executed nowhere, the index up
to the implemented isomorphism: its rectangle counts sequences, not
classes, and whether the first count is an invariant of the isomorphism
class was neither proved nor computed there.

The seed's rule for choosing a rung takes the smallest named leftover
that either reduces the count of declared inputs or extends the census of
the seed's challenge five, and on a tie the one named first.  Rung
three's own application of that rule, recorded at the end of its
`STATUS.md`, selects X-3: it gives a second reading of the census row for
succession and bears on the row for symmetry.

## The objects

The sealed isomorphism relation relates two regimes by a pair of
bijections, one on datum indices and one on form indices, that preserves
arities and carries the admitted instances, rules and requirements onto
one another.  Presentation tokens are not part of it.  The sealed Python
decides it by a key procedure and cross-checks that procedure against a
brute-force minimum over every bijection pair; the sealed Lean states it
as a structure of membership conditions.

A stake is one of the seven rung three reads: the stake labelling every
pair free, the five two-sided stakes the sibling commitment tree
declares, and the stake the one-sided total commitment induces.  A
stake is UNIFORM when it assigns one and the same label to every pair of
distinct indices.

Two histories, each from its own base, are CLASS-MATCHED when, at every
count, either both prefixes reach regimes that the isomorphism relates,
or both prefixes reach none.

## The question

- *One.*  Given two regimes the isomorphism relates, and a stake, must
  both carry a demand or both carry none?  For which of the seven stakes;
  is uniformity enough, and is anything weaker enough?
- *Two.*  Where the answer to one is not everywhere yes, is there a
  regime reached in the frozen rectangle whose isomorphism class, among
  the regimes reached, holds one carrying a demand and one carrying none?
  And whose class, taken as every renaming of a reached regime, holds
  both?
- *Three.*  Must two class-matched histories have the same first count,
  under a uniform stake?  Under each of the other stakes?  If not, by
  what witness, and at what length and what count of data and forms does
  the first witness appear?
- *Four.*  Over the frozen rectangle, quotient the sequences of all three
  bases by the class of their whole prefix trajectory.  Under each stake,
  on how many classes is the first count not constant?  How many classes
  have more than one member, so that the answer is not vacuous?
- *Five.*  Is the first count a function of the isomorphism class of the
  regime the whole sequence reaches?  Over the rectangle, on how many
  such classes does it vary?
- *Six.*  Does renaming the presentation tokens a history carries --
  injectively, over the declared token alphabet -- ever move the first
  count, or the isomorphism class of any prefix?
- *Seven.*  At every regime reached in the rectangle, does the sealed key
  procedure give the same partition into classes as the brute-force
  minimum, and does the sealed automorphism count equal a brute-force
  count over every bijection pair?  What sizes does the automorphism
  group take?

No answer to any of the seven is stated here and none is expected.  Each
is closed by a proof, by an executable countermodel, or by an explicit
OPEN status.

The word "free" appears above, and appears throughout this rung, only as
the name of one of the three values a stake assigns to a pair.  It is
never the guarded adjective, which this rung writes nowhere.

## What is reconstructed and what is native

The sealed kernel's regime, contexts, derivability, collapse,
separability, event kinds, step and replay are reconstructed verbatim
from the sealed sources, as rung three did.  The sealed isomorphism
structure, its identity instance, and the transport of collapse across
it are reconstructed verbatim from the two sealed modules that state
them.  The label, the stake, honour and the demand predicate are
reconstructed verbatim from the track-one sibling rung, and the prefix
reading, carrying a demand and being a first count are reconstructed
verbatim from rung three.  Each reconstruction carries a pin note and is
compared declaration by declaration with its pinned source.  Nothing
sealed and no sibling package is imported by the Lean of this rung, and
no sibling source is copied into the Python of this rung: the sealed
kernel, the sealed isomorphism procedure and the sibling computations are
consumed read-only through their public functions, pinned by blob.

This rung's own objects are the transport of separability and of a
demand across the isomorphism, uniformity, class-matching, and whatever
the seven questions above require beyond them.

## The finite cross-check

The rectangle is rung three's, unchanged: the same three bases, the same
declared alphabet, every sequence of at most four declared events, run
without pruning.  Every regime reached is keyed by the sealed procedure
and by the brute-force minimum; every renaming of every class
representative is formed by the sealed renaming helper and replayed
nowhere, because a renaming is a presentation of a class and not an
admitted state.  Every node is replayed from its base.  Every bound and
every count is declared in `PREDICTIONS.md` before the first computation
and recorded in the receipt afterwards.

## Discipline

Every substantive line carries exactly one evidence class.  A target true
by unfolding a definition is recorded as being at the level of a
definition, in those words, and is never counted as content.  No theorem
is stated without its grammar in the same sentence.  Predictions are
frozen before the first computation and before the first compile, scored
mechanically, and never edited.  A receipt is not a proof.  A pin proves
only that a source did not move.  A gate is proved by failing on the
defect it exists to catch, with the failure naming the injected object,
and by recovering when the injection is reversed.  Nothing of the sealed
estate's standing obstructions is treated here as anything other than
standing.
