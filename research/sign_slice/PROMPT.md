# 19L57G1 research run -- a finite slice of the section classifier

Run this as one autonomous construction run on merged `main`.

A caller supplied an external classification challenge through the
operator channel.  Its native translation, which is the ONLY form used
anywhere in this tree:

- the supplied objects become **GenPres**: a finite generator
  presentation -- objects, declared arrows with per-arrow transports,
  an observer per object, a weighting, a declared generator list, a
  declared forest, and the occurrence-slot list;
- the supplied success notion becomes **ConeSection**: the GenPres
  admits a natural section, decided by the section criterion already
  proved in the natural-sections packet (generator form and holonomy
  form), and by nothing else;
- the supplied totality ("the set of all") is **REFUSED** as a
  completed object and replaced by **SliceSet**, a finite declared
  census, plus **Register**, the obstruction register of the census's
  complement.

Five steps, in order, no step assuming a unique survivor from a step
that still carries a tie:

1. **GenPres** -- declare the finite grammar, its cost measure, its
   iso key; admit or refuse the classes G0..G5 with witnesses or
   countermodels.
2. **ConeSection** -- implement the decision predicate by the cited
   iff theorems; dispose the five pinned predictions.
3. **SliceSet** -- a finite declared census under declared bounds;
   classify every member SECTIONED or OBSTRUCTED by the predicate;
   pin the partition.
4. **OccRelabel** -- price the first convention class strictly larger
   than the proved pointwise re-presentation invariance: one map per
   occurrence slot, not one per object.  Decide, inside a declared
   bound, whether the class can move.
5. **Register** -- one inspectable row per obstructed member: witness
   walk or named bound-hit, mover count, twisted against total,
   repair-mass applicability, the occurrence-relabel outcome, and the
   priced leftover.

Hard rules of this tree: no sealed path is written; no supplied
challenge vocabulary and no outside theory name appears in any
committed file; the working denylist stays out; the three protected
opens (OB-1, S6 item 6, S9 last bullet) stay open; ties are retained;
a table is not a computation; a green test discharges nothing by
itself; the complete classification is not a legal terminal.

Every substantive line carries exactly one evidence class.  Every
theorem-level sentence is a citation into the pinned sibling packet or
is downgraded; this run derives no new theorem-level claims of its
own.  Every computational claim has deterministic, fully typed Python
evidence with byte-replayable receipts.  Predictions are written and
hashed before the first computation of their phase; a missed
prediction is recorded next to its value, never edited.
