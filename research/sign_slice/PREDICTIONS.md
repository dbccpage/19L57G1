# Predictions, written and hashed before each phase's first computation

A missed prediction is recorded next to its value and never edited.

## Phase 1: the grammar (written before any grammar computation)

- PA1: G0 (objects, no arrows) IS a GenPres; with the default observer
  at two labels it is SECTIONED trivially (no arrow constrains the
  choice); cheapest witness is the one-object presentation.
- PA2: G1 (arrows, no weighting) is NOT a GenPres: the weighting field
  is on the bill and absent; the countermodel is the tuple itself.
- PA3: G2 (unit weighting) and G3 (signed integer weighting) are
  GenPres classes with inhabitants at the minimum of the declared
  measure muG = (object count, arrow count, distinct amp values),
  lexicographic.
- PA4: G4 reconstructions (chain, square, star, ladder, square-plus-edge
  configuration families at occupancies 2 and 3) inhabit the grammar
  wherever the object bound admits them; the occupancy-2 ladder
  exceeds the object bound and is REFUSED by name, not silently
  dropped.
- PA5: G5 (the two-coordinate signed-square pair, reconstructed) is a
  pair of grammar inhabitants sharing squared amps and differing in
  sign, separated by the declared mixing read-out.

## Phase 2: the predicate (written before any predicate computation)

- PB1: every reconstructed chain fixture that the sibling packet
  recorded as "section exists" satisfies ConeSection.
- PB2: every reconstructed square and star fixture the packet recorded
  as "no section" fails ConeSection.
- PB3: pointwise re-presentation invariance applies: ConeSection is
  unmoved by any pointwise per-object re-presentation, checked
  computationally on declared samples and cited to the packet's
  proved invariance for the general statement.
- PB4: if ConeSection holds, twisted equals total at the section pair
  for every declared walk list (cited: the packet's A1), checked on
  declared samples.
- PB5: twisted equals total does NOT imply ConeSection: the packet's
  square countermodel is reconstructed and re-verified here.

## Phase 4: the occurrence relabel (written before the exhaustion ran)

- PD1: every pointwise-shaped assignment leaves the class unmoved --
  the packet's proved invariance, checked by reconstruction on the
  declared samples.
- PD2: on the two-label square, the full slot-map space under the
  declared bound (identity or exchange per slot) is exhausted, and
  whether any assignment flips the class is reported from the
  exhaustion alone.
- PD3: if no assignment flips, the terminal is the bounded invariance,
  never a grammar-wide claim.
- PD4: if some assignment flips, the terminal names the witness and
  the price, and the packet's pointwise invariance is not contradicted
  because the witness is not pointwise-constant.
