1. Supply is not admission. A glyph in front of you changes nothing until an
   admission event changes state.

2. Presentation tokens are forgettable. Internal identity, when there is any,
   is an index in an admitted list, not the ink.

3. Two data are internally separable only if something admitted distinguishes
   them: either (i) a context/hole whose plug differs, or (ii) no automorphism
   carries one index to the other. Anything only the metatheory can see is NOT
   internal.

4. Let σ(x,y)=(y,x) when a pair of admitted contents exists.
   Orientation is present exactly when σ is not an automorphism of the
   admitted structure. Equivalently: some admitted witness D has D(x,y)≠D(y,x).
   These two routes are NOT the same mechanism and must be kept distinct:
     (A) admit a non-symmetric witness (relation r, projection L, named sides
         that are ADMITTED as structure);
     (B) withdraw σ from the automorphism family without adding any witness.
   You must not equate (A) and (B). You must also decide, as Lemma L1 below,
   whether (B) is a coherent, non-empty mechanism at all.

5. Nothing in a bare boundary forces <rab> (a non-symmetric relation on two
   contents). Equivariant / swap-invariant structure reaches at most the
   unordered pair. A one-way edge is an extra admission.

6. The word "container" was never derived. Do not use it as a sort. Speak
   of the bar, of sides, of names, of lists, of automorphisms.

7. No clock has been defined. You may not solve TIME by naming a clock.

8. NAMING IS NOT ORIENTATION. Naming the two sides of the bar is a reading
   applied with ZERO contents; σ is not even defined until a pair of contents
   exists. Do not treat named empty sides as orientation, and do not treat "the
   names hold at genesis" as "σ∉Aut at genesis." Orientation (σ∉Aut) is defined
   only once two admitted contents exist.

9. STATIC TWO-SIDEDNESS IS NOT SUCCESSION. Two named or occupied sides
   (a static "near/far") is one thing. A succession (a "before/after" that is
   itself an admitted object, an order on events available internally) is a
   different, stronger thing. Do not treat a static near/far as a before/after,
   and do not treat "orientation" as automatically "succession."
