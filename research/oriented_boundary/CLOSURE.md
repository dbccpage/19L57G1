## The resolution, in the repo's own terms

**1. The (B)-is-empty lemma** (the positive result the obstruction lacked).
Two routes to `σ ∉ Aut` were held distinct: (A) admit a non-symmetric witness
(a relation, a projection, an observable occupancy); (B) withdraw `σ` from the
automorphism family with **no** witness. Claim: **(B) is empty.**
`Aut(M)` is, by definition, the permutations preserving every admitted judgment.
If no admitted witness `D` distinguishes `x, y`, then every admitted `D` has
`D(x,y) = D(y,x)`, so `σ(x,y) = (y,x)` preserves every judgment and
`σ ∈ Aut(M)`. Hence `σ ∉ Aut(M)` can occur **only** if some admitted `D` has
`D(x,y) ≠ D(y,x)` — which is exactly (A). A "withdrawal" of `σ` with no such `D`
is either a metatheoretic stipulation — the forbidden **observer from nowhere** —
or a covert (A). So (B) does not exist as a distinct mechanism.

**2. What an order *is*** (the deferred classification, now made).
By R22 the admission order is **not internally available** as a readable
sequence — reversing two identical supplies is not a change any admitted
observer can notice. So order-as-bare-sequence separates nothing internally; the
separation that side-free *does* achieve turns on an admitted distinguisher, a
non-symmetric witness — mechanism (A), priced at **exactly one capability**. Its
kind is therefore fixed: **an order is a symmetry-breaking admitted relation**,
not free sequence and not a from-nowhere stipulation.

**3. An order is not a container.** A container — the boundary — **exists before
its contents** (empty sides at genesis, zero marks). An order/relation exists
**only with** its contents; it needs a pair before it can hold. Different
existence conditions ⇒ **different kinds**. Equivalently, in the seed's own
types, a boundary is a slot; an order is a relation admitted on top of one. So
"is the admission order a container?" — **no.** It is a paid symmetry-breaking
witness, not a pre-existing slot.

**4. And it is not time.** Even orientation (`σ ∉ Aut`) is a **static**
two-sidedness, not a **succession** — a before/after admitted as an internal
object. The language reaches the static one and never the successive one. So the
"secretly a container / secretly time" reading closes twice over: an order is a
witness (not a container), and succession (the only honest meaning of "time")
never entered `<>`, `<|>`, `<a|b>` at all.
