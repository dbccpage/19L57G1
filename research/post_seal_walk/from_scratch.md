### 1. Second inscription `<|>` (boundary_seed)

A *different* initial object, not a commentary on `<>`. Admit one
primitive, priced as one declaration: a **boundary with two sides**. Carry
two readings to the end:

- **U** — sides distinct, unnamed. Reflection is a renaming the object
  language cannot see.
- **O** — sides named (near / far). The object language can tell them
  apart.

Challenge 0: empty sides are **not** an internal fact. No successor is
forced. Under U the empty sides are collapsed; under O they differ only
by the two admitted names.

Challenge 3: require `<a|>` and `<|a>` internally separable.

- Under **O** the current regime already meets it (cost **0**). What did
  the work is **withdrawal of the side exchange, paid at genesis**. The
  two names are idle.
- Under **U** it fails. Minimum cost **1**, **three** tied classes:
  withdraw the side exchange; withdraw mark permutations (meets by
  unfolding — the boundary is idle); one further mark, either side.

So: a container at genesis is an admitted primitive, not a derivation.
Named sides buy the requirement for free. Unnamed sides do not.

Provenance of the existing run: `SOURCE_ISOLATED_WITH_DECLARED_PRIOR_EXPOSURE`,
not a blind-discovery receipt. A from-scratch rebuild would freeze that
ceiling in the seed *before* the first session, or run it with an
instrument that has not seen `<>`.

### 3. What kind of thing is an order (oriented_boundary)

The missing kind-classification (`SF-OPEN-A`).

Load-bearing lemma, proved: **(B) is empty**. `σ ∈ Aut` iff no admitted
judgment distinguishes the pair. `σ ∉ Aut` **forces** an admitted
asymmetric witness (mechanism A). You cannot withdraw the swap from the
automorphism family with no witness. That would be an observer from
nowhere.

Unoriented reading: swap is an automorphism (one symmetric judgment).
Oriented reading: swap breaks at **exactly one** asymmetric witness.

Kind, analytic on existence conditions:

- A **boundary** exists **before** its contents (empty sides at genesis).
- An **order / relation** exists **only with** its contents (needs a pair).

Different existence conditions, different kinds. The admission order is
**not** a container. It is a paid symmetry-breaking admitted witness.

Even orientation (`σ ∉ Aut`) is **static two-sidedness**, not succession.
No clock, no before/after as an internal object, entered `<>`, `<|>`, or
`<a|b>`.

The author's withheld reading of the seed (unoriented / oriented as two
imported nouns) is in `AUTHOR_READING.md` and is **refused as a claim
about nature**. The object-language result is the kind split and the
empty (B). The blind `SEED.md` of this tree is still parked for an
independent confirmation.

### 5. Growth under a standing commitment (standing_commitment)

A local strategy *emits* a next demand. A standing commitment does not:
it is declared once, says which pairs must stay apart when both are
held, and emits nothing.

Under the total-apart commitment, one growth step on `n` data adds
exactly `n` demands, with **no act between the growth and the demand**.
A discharged fixpoint is not absorbing: growth reopens it.

This does **not** close `OB-1`. Nothing here *produces* a commitment.
Growth events remain external. What changes is accounting: many imposed
requirements collapse to one standing declaration.
