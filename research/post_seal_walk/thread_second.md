## Standing commitment — `standing_commitment/`

A local strategy emits a next demand. A standing commitment does not.
It is declared once: which pairs stay apart when both are held.

Under `total`, one growth step on `n` data adds exactly `n` demands.
**No act occurs between the growth and the demand.** A discharged
fixpoint is not absorbing (`fixpoint_reopens`). Empty commitment
generates nothing.

Does **not** close `OB-1`. Nothing produces a commitment. Growth remains
external. What changes is accounting: many imposed requirements become
one standing declaration.

Lean: `growth_generates`, `demand_count_grows`,
`empty_commitment_generates_nothing`. Reconstructs `Represented.lean`
under pin; repair machinery deliberately omitted.
