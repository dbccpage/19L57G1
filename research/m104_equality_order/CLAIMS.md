# Claims Ledger: Target M104 / M107 (Coherent Equality Layers & Order Family)

## 1. What it supports

1. **Four Separated Equality Layers**:
   Demonstrates in this formal setting the strict distinction between:
   - Presentation Identity (syntactic/token equality)
   - Definitional Equality (canonical normal form equality)
   - Propositional/Path Equality (witnessed identity type or derivation path)
   - Observational Equivalence (projection equality under all admitted observers)
2. **Strict Hierarchy and Non-Implication Witnesses**:
   Exhibits concrete finite witnesses demonstrating that definitional equality does not imply presentation identity ($1+2 \ne 3$ as terms), and observational equivalence does not imply carrier state identity (identical visible projections over distinct hidden sectors).
3. **Quotient Representative Independence**:
   Demonstrates that legal operations and prices evaluated on canonical quotient representatives do not depend on the choice of presentation representative.
4. **Three Structurally Distinct Derived Orders**:
   Derives and separates:
   - Reachability order ($\le_{\mathrm{reach}}$, a preorder on transition states)
   - Observational refinement order ($\le_{\mathrm{obs}}$, an information poset on observer projections)
   - Cost order ($\le_{\mathrm{cost}}$, a quasi-order on traces via exact rational costs in $\mathbb{Q}$)
5. **Non-Conflation of Orders**:
   Shows that reachability between states does not determine trace cost, and observer refinement does not determine state transition order.

## 2. What it does not support

1. Does not claim higher homotopy type theory or the univalence axiom for infinite types.
2. Does not claim infinite continuous spectral orders or general topology on infinite sets.
3. Does not prove completeness of arbitrary equational theories.

## 3. The narrowest defensible claim

Under the declared syntactic and discrete state models, the four equality layers form a strict hierarchy with concrete separation witnesses, quotient evaluations are representative-invariant, and reachability, refinement, and cost define distinct, non-conflated order structures.
