## 1. Honest Boundaries & Non-Claims

1. **Ties Stay Ties (No King Delta)**:
   Under $\mu_1$, the minimal valid responses to an unseparated pair are tied across
   multiple non-isomorphic branches. This module exposes the full menu `deltas: tuple[Delta, ...]`
   and **does not** automatically select `deltas[0]`. Selecting from a tie requires an
   external declaration or operator action.

2. **5-Atom Collapse is an Exhibit, Not a Uniqueness Proof**:
   The 5-atom construction (2 data, 1 binary form, 1 instance, 1 irreflexivity rule) is an
   *exhibit* of a finite object where identifying quotient collapse violates an admitted law.
   It proves sufficiency/existence, not that a 4-atom or 3-atom failure is impossible under
   all conceivable grammars.

3. **Obstruction Gates are a Diagnostic Fixture**:
   The 4-cell $\text{obstP}$ gate checker evaluates whether the conditions (Retained, Protected,
   Declared-Observation, Observer-Zero) are satisfied. In this packet, these bits are evaluated as
   a diagnostic fixture; they are not an endogenous derivation of authority.

4. **Genesis Quiescence**:
   The empty regime `EMPTY` with no active requirements does not transition. Dynamics is
   conditional on the presentation of an unseparated requirement and a declared response menu.
