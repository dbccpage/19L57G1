## The measure, declared

    REACH(base, delta) = the number of pluggings that become derivable.

Nothing is weighted by hand. A plugging is the estate's own unit of
observable content, and a datum's profile is the set of contexts derivable
for it, so reach is the growth of total profile size. `mu3 = (reach, item
count)`, lexicographic, reach leading.
